# Film ideas (our own stories, same quiet 2D vibe)

Format: nameless hero, an inner voice, a day counter, setbacks, a turning point, a quiet hopeful end, few words, money/discipline theme.

1. **The Quiet Jar** (made: 101-the-quiet-jar.json) - small treats leak his coins away; one coin in an empty jar starts a new count; a broken car empties the jar; he starts again smaller and opens a tiny shop.

2. **The Blank Page** - A man wants to learn a paid skill (drawing signs/lettering). Every night the phone wins. The voice says "someone better already exists." He does 10 minutes a night, day counter climbs; a bad first sale (nobody buys), he keeps the notebook; ends with his first handmade sign hanging over a small door. Sets: desk, couch, store, office, mirror. Hope: his ruined first sign kept on the wall.

3. **The Stall** - Starting a tiny business: he makes one small thing at the kitchen table (fridge, desk sets) and sets up a stall (store set) where nobody stops for many days; rain washes the day out; the voice says "pack it up." He comes back with one price tag changed and one smile; the first customer is a child with a single coin. Ending: the stall light turning on at dusk.
