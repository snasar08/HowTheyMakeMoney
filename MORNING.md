# Update, Nov 11

You told me to download the better free character models myself, so I did. The animated Shorts now use real modelled people instead of one recoloured mannequin: 8 men and 6 women from Quaternius's free "Ultimate Modular Men/Women" packs (public domain, about 20 MB, credits in `assets/characters/CREDITS.md`), each with a face, hair and proper clothes, and the heads, bodies, legs and feet can be swapped and recoloured. Famous people are built from those parts (Ronaldo's spiky hair, Jordan bald with a goatee, LeBron with a buzz cut, beard and headband, Messi's beard, Taylor Swift's blonde bob and dress), still with plain colours and no logos, and crowds are now 16 different people. I ruled out two other packs first: the smooth one is underwear-only in its free version, and the cute mini one is blockier than what we had. It was tested on GitHub: Blender look checks of six Shorts, all 20 animated scripts rendered without errors, and a full dry run of the Ronaldo Short with the real voice (all 20 Blender machines passed, pictures in `previews/`), then merged to main. What did not work at first: the first Blender test showed frozen people with the headband behind the head, because Blender names the animations differently; that is fixed. Later fixes the same day: LeBron's beard is now a real modelled beard, block stubble is gone, a goatee that vanished in some standing shots is fixed, and Ronaldo gets his spiky hair in full renders (an old setting was overriding it). Spiky hair now keeps its points in Blender (confirmed by a second full dry run of the Ronaldo Short). I tried a drawn mouth and dropped it: as a block it sank into the face or looked stern. Still simple: the faces, which is the pack's style.
