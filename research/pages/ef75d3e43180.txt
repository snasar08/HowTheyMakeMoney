Ja’Marr Chase makes light of Joe Burrow’s illness with Bengals jersey swap

Cincinnati Bengals wide receiver Ja'Marr Chase poked fun at Joe Burrow after he missed his media availability.

By Jackson Stone•Nov 19, 2026 at 3:23 PM ET•2 min read

Add ClutchPoints on Google

Kirby Lee-Imagn Images

The Cincinnati Bengals are currently getting ready for their upcoming game on the road vs the Washington Commanders on Monday evening. The Bengals are hoping to continue their winning ways after dismantling the Pittsburgh Steelers over the weekend, taking control of the AFC North in the process.

On Thursday, it was revealed that star quarterback Joe Burrow had missed that day's media availability due to an illness, although he was still able to participate in practice.

Star wide receiver Ja'Marr Chase found a creative way to make light of the situation at his own presser.

“Ja’Marr Chase walks into the press conference in Burrow’s spot wearing his No. 9 jersey,” reported Paul Dehner Jr. of The Athletic on X, formerly Twitter.

Chase and Burrow have had a great working relationship dating back to their days as college stars for the LSU Tigers, and that has continued onto the NFL level, as they now form arguably the best quarterback-receiver duo in the league today.

Burrow passed for over 300 yards in the first half alone last week vs the Steelers, with many of those passes going to Chase, who has broken through as the clear top receiver in the league this year.

Meanwhile, the Bengals' defense has also mostly been up to the challenge this season after being among the worst units in the league over the last few years, making Cincinnati look poised to reclaim their stake as a legit title contender in the AFC playoff picture.

In any case, kickoff between the Bengals and Commanders is set for Monday evening at 8:15 pm ET from Landover. The game will be carried by ESPN.
