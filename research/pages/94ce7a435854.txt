Karol G brings No Me Arrepiento De Sentir Tanto to TikTok LIVE

Karol G will perform songs from her new album, No Me Arrepiento De Sentir Tanto, in an hour-long, all-LIVE set as part of TikTok LIVE Premiere.Her community of 56 million followers on TikTok have made over 1.7 million videos with “Si Antes Te Hubiera Conocido” alone.Watch on November 4, 8:00–9:00 PM CDMX time, at @karolg.Karol G (@karolg) is taking her new album live on TikTok. On November 4, from 8:00–9:00 PM CDMX time, she’ll perform music from No Me Arrepiento De Sentir Tanto in a one-hour set for TikTok LIVE Premiere, streamed straight to fans wherever they are.The album arrived in August through Bichota Records and Interscope and went to No. 1 on Billboard’s Top Latin Albums chart. It’s a heartbreak record, and a big one, with guest spots from Drake, Bruno Mars, Judeline, Rusowsky, and Greg Gonzalez of Cigarettes After Sex.TikTok has been part of Karol G’s rise for years, and her community has more 56 million people following. “Si Antes Te Hubiera Conocido” was one of the songs of summer 2024 and has since turned up in more than 1.7 million videos. When she released Tropicoqueta in June 2025, fans got a full in-app campaign around it: an AI Karol G effect, themed profile frames, and a leaderboard ranking countries by views of Tropicoqueta content.Eduardo Praça, TikTok Artist Partnership US Latin & Latam, said: “Latin artists are among the most powerful drivers of music discovery on TikTok, and Karol G is at the center of that movement. Her fans on TikTok don’t just listen, they create, celebrate and show up for every chapter of her journey. With TikTok LIVE we’re bringing together creativity, fandom, and live connection to give fans a front row seat to their favorite artists' biggest moments.”TikTok LIVE Premiere is TikTok’s flagship LIVE series, built for the biggest names in music, entertainment, sports, and culture to mark their major moments with exclusive livestreams.Because it’s LIVE, the comments run right alongside the performance. A fan in Medellín and a fan in Madrid are watching the same set, at the same moment, and reacting together.Tune in on Karol G’s official TikTok account and participating TikTok handles on November 4 at 8:00–9:00 PM CDMX time.
