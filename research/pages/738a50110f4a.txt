NFL, UFL and CFL minimum salaries for 2026

The NFL's minimum salary in 2026 is $885,000 for a rookie, rising with each credited season to $1,300,000 for a player of seven or more. A practice squad player is paid at least $13,750 a week, a UFL player at least $64,000 for the season, and a CFL player at least CA$70,000.

Frequently asked questions
 What is the NFL minimum salary in 2026? 
 The NFL minimum salary in 2026 is $885,000 for a rookie, $1,005,000 for a player with one credited season, $1,075,000 with two, $1,145,000 with three, $1,215,000 with four to six and $1,300,000 with seven or more. 
 How much are NFL practice squad players paid? 
 A practice squad player is paid at least $13,750 a week in 2026. A veteran on one of a club's limited veteran places is paid at least $18,350 and at most $22,850. 
 What will the NFL minimum salary be in 2030? 
 The agreement sets the 2030 minimum at $1,065,000 for a rookie and $1,480,000 for a player of seven or more credited seasons. 
 What is the UFL minimum salary? 
 The UFL minimum salary is $64,000 in 2026, paid as $6,400 a game over the 10-game regular season. 
 What is the CFL minimum salary? 
 The CFL minimum salary is CA$70,000 in 2026, which is $49,976, and it rises to CA$75,000 in 2027.
