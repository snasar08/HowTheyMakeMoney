                              Domino’s 101: Fun Facts
                              PIZZA, PRODUCT & MENU
                               • There are more than 34 million ways to create a single Domino’s pizza.
                               • Pepperoni is the most popular Domino’s pizza topping in the U.S., followed
                                 by sausage, bacon, pineapple and ham.
                               • Breadsticks were the first national nonpizza menu item offered at Domino’s,
                                 in 1992.
                               • Domino’s World’s Fastest Pizza Maker Richard Delcid can make three
                                 large pizzas in just 31.22 seconds!

                              STORE & FRANCHISE OWNER FACTS
                               • About 98% of Domino’s stores in the U.S. are franchise-owned.
                               • Domino’s has about 750 independent franchise owners in the U.S.
                                   • More than 95% started their careers as drivers, pizza makers or
                                     hourly workers.

                              BUSIEST DAYS FOR DOMINO’S
                               • Halloween, New Year’s Eve and Valentine's Day are typically three of
                                 Domino’s busiest days of the year.
                               • Major sporting events, such as March's big basketball tournament, also
                                 tend to drive higher order volumes at Domino’s locations nationwide.
                               • Domino’s in the U.S. typically sells around 2.4 million pizzas on football's
                                 biggest Sunday of the year – about 40% more than on a normal Sunday.

                              TECHNOLOGY & ONLINE
                               • Domino’s AnyWare is a suite of ordering technology that gives customers
                                 various ways to order digitally – allowing them to order from anywhere, at
                                 any time, using whatever device they’d like.
                               • In the U.S. in 2025, Domino’s generated more than 85% of retail sales via
                                 digital channels.
