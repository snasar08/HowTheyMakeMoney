About Spotify

Since its launch in 2008, Spotify has revolutionised music listening. Our move into podcasting brought innovation and a new generation of listeners to the medium. In 2022, we took the next leap, entering the fast-growing audiobook market—continuing to shape the future of audio.

Today, more listeners than ever can discover, manage and enjoy over 100 million tracks, 8 million podcast titles, and 800,000 audiobooks in select markets on Spotify. We are the world’s most popular audio streaming subscription service with 788 million users, including 306 million subscribers, in 184 markets.
