Spotify Q3 2026: 788M Users and 306M Subscribers

Spotify stock jumped after a Q3 2026 report showed strong growth with 788M monthly users and 306M premium subscribers alongside record margins.

[UPDATED] Spotify released its Q3 2026 subscriber and earnings report showing sustained double-digit growth while also improving profitability. The streaming giant met or exceeded analyst predictions across most key performance indicators.

Wall Street reacted positively. Spotify stock rose 5% Thursday in premarket trading - a welcome rebound from a 6% drop so far this year.

Spotify [SPOT] closed the day at $583.66 up 11.19%, but still well below its October 2025 high of $673.86. 

Following the Q3 report several major analysts raised their price targets including Morgan Stanley to $700, Raymond James to $625, Guggenheim to $650 and Evercore ISI to $750.

Here is the report breakdown:

The User Stats: MAUs & Subscribers

Spotify continues to expand its global listener base, showcasing strong double-digit growth at the top of the funnel.

Total Monthly Active Users (MAUs) reached 788 million.
11% year-over-year increase.

11 million net new MAUs.

Premium Subscribers grew to 306 million.
9% year-over-year growth.

6 million net premium subscriber additions during the quarter.

Ad-Supported MAUs rose to 499 million.
a 12% increase year-over-year for the ad-supported tier.

Financial Breakdown: Revenue & Profitability

Spotify's financial performance highlights mid-teens revenue growth combined with scaling efficiency.

Total Revenue expanded to €4.969 billion/$5,569 billion
16% expansion year-over-year.

Premium Revenue contributed €4.487 billion, which is a 17% jump year-over-year.

Ad-Supported Revenue accounted for €482 million, a 10% year-over-year increase.

Gross Margin finished at a Q3 record high of 33.0%.

Creator and Platform Expansions

Beyond the core numbers, Spotify also scaled several personalized audio experiences and creator monetization tools globally.

Spotify launched Creator Memberships in 34 markets.
This tool allows eligible podcast creators to offer paid memberships directly to their fans to generate recurring revenue.

Analysts are speculating that Creator Memberships will expand to music in 2027

The Spotify Partner Program expanded to more than 35 new markets.
This expansion gives podcast creators in countries like Brazil, Mexico, Italy, and Spain new opportunities to earn revenue based on engagement.

A new Taste Profile was launched in beta for Premium Subscribers in the U.S., giving users an editable view of how the platform understands their taste.

Audiobooks are scheduled to expand globally from 22 markets to more than 180 by year-end 2026.

Hypebot’s Bottom Line

Spotify’s Q3 2026 results prove that the streaming giant is successfully code on scaling profitability without sacrificing audience growth. 

Posting 11% year-over-year MAU growth to hit 788 million users while simultaneously achieving a Q3 record high gross margin of 33.0% is a solid victory. 

For the music industry, the aggressive global expansion of the Spotify Partner Program and the rollout of Creator Memberships signal that the platform is working to diversify how podcast creators can monetize their fanbases outside of traditional music royalty streams. Musicians are waiting their turn.
