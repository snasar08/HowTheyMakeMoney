Connor McDavid Ties Wayne Gretzky's Record for Fewest Games to 40 Assists

Sergey Demidov Head of News Department

Fact checked by: Sol Fayerman-Hansen

Updated: Nov 16, 2026, 5:27 a.m.Published: Nov 16, 2026, 5:27 a.m.

4 min read

Editorial StandardsWhy Trust Us?

Edmonton Oilers captain Connor McDavid tied Wayne Gretzky's NHL record for the fewest games to reach 40 assists in a season, getting there in his 22nd game Sunday against the Philadelphia Flyers.

(Photo by Emilee Chinn/Getty Images)

McDavid took a pass from Leon Draisaitl in the slot and sent a backhand feed back to his linemate for a first-period power-play goal, Sportsnet reported. Gretzky also recorded his 40th assist in his 22nd game, in the 1984-85 season.

McDavid finished with a goal and two assists in a 5-2 win, giving him 12 goals and 41 assists for 53 points. Draisaitl scored twice to reach 20 goals, and Edmonton improved to 16-3-3.

Point Streak Reaches 22 Games

McDavid has a point in all 22 games this season, a career-best streak that ties Dany Heatley's 22-game run in 2005-06 for the fifth-longest season-opening point streak in NHL history.

Gretzky's 51-game point streak from the start of the 1983-84 season remains the NHL record. He had 61 goals and 92 assists during that run and finished the season with 205 points.

McDavid reached 50 points with an assist on Saturday in a 1-0 win over the Toronto Maple Leafs. Earlier this season, he became the fastest player to 30 assists, needing 15 games to break Gretzky's mark of 16.

Chasing 200

Gretzky is the only player in NHL history to reach 200 points in a season, doing it four times with the Oilers. His record is 215 points in 1985-86.
