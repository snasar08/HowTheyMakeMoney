Lego products are divided in series of sets known as themes, which are based around a specific license or concept. This article contains an inexhaustive list of themes released by The Lego Group, both currently produced and discontinued. Many themes are also based on preexisting licensed properties or media franchises.

Current themes
[edit]

Original
[edit]

Lego Architecture: A product line featuring small-scale recreations of real buildings.[1]

Lego Art: A product line featuring artistic designs, often featuring mosaic-like portraits designed using small tiles.[2][3]

Lego Botanicals

Lego BrickHeadz: A product line featuring large buildable figurines, often based on licensed properties.[4][5]

Lego City: A brand of sets taking place within a large city environment. The product line contains many subthemes. It was previously known as Lego Town until 2003, and as Lego World City from 2003 to 2004.[6]

Lego Classic

Lego Creator: A Lego theme featuring generic models. Sets released Creator 3in1 allow multiple builds to be created with the same set.[7]

BrickLink Designer Program: A crowdfunding program ran through BrickLink where sets designed by Lego fans are produced for a limited time.[8]

Lego Duplo

Lego Editions

Lego Education

Lego Friends

Lego Icons: A Lego theme featuring more advanced models intended for older audiences. The product line includes the Modular Buildings series, featuring large connectable models of urban buildings. It was known as Lego Creator Expert until 2020.[9][10]

Lego Ideas

Lego Minifigures

Lego Monkie Kid

Lego Ninjago

Lego Speed Champions: A racing theme introduced in 2015 featuring models of vehicles from well-known car brands.[11]

Lego Technic: A Lego theme featuring sets designed using unique gears primarily consisting of machines and vehicles. It was introduced in 1977.[12][13]

Licensed
[edit]

Lego Animal Crossing: A Lego theme introduced in 2024 based off the Animal Crossing video game series.[14]

Lego Bluey: A Lego theme released in 2025 based on the television series Bluey.[15]

Lego DC Super Heroes (Batman): A product line based on the DC Comics superhero universe, including many sets featuring the superhero Batman.[16]

Lego Disney: A Lego theme featuring sets based on various Disney properties as well as the Disney Princess line.[17]

Lego Dune

Lego Fortnite

Lego Gabby's Dollhouse: A Lego theme released in 2023 based on the Gabby's Dollhouse television series.[18]

Lego Marvel Super Heroes (Spider-Man): A Lego theme featuring sets based on the Marvel Comics superhero universe.[19]

Lego Minecraft: A series of sets first introduced in 2012 based on the sandbox game Minecraft.[20]

Lego Nike

Lego One Piece

Lego Shrek: A product line based on DreamWorks Animation's Shrek franchise.[21]

Licensed
[edit]

Lego The Angry Birds Movie: A product line released in 2016 based on The Angry Birds Movie, itself based on Rovio Entertainment's Angry Birds video game franchise.[39]

Lego Avatar

Lego Avatar: The Last Airbender: A Lego theme based on the Nickelodeon television series. Two sets were released.[40][41]

The Lego Batman Movie

Lego Ben 10: Alien Force

Lego Cars

Lego DC Super Hero Girls: A Lego theme introduced in 2017 based on the DC Super Hero Girls web series. It was retired in 2018.[42][43]

Lego Despicable Me

Lego Ghostbusters

Lego Indiana Jones: A product line based on the Indiana Jones film franchise available from 2008 to 2009.[44] It was reintroduced in 2023.[45]

Lego Mickey Mouse

Lego Minions

Lego Overwatch

Lego Pirates of the Caribbean

Lego Prince of Persia

Lego Scooby-Doo: A product line introduced in 2015 based off Warner Bros. Scooby-Doo franchise.[46] It was retired in 2016.[47]

Lego Speed Racer: A series of sets based on the 2008 film.[48]

Lego SpongeBob SquarePants: A product line released from 2006 to 2012 based on the animated television series. It was reintroduced as a LEGO Icons set in 2026.[49]

Lego Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles: A product line available from 2012 to 2014 based on the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles franchise.[50][51]

Lego The Lone Ranger:

Lego The Powerpuff Girls: A Lego theme released in 2018 based on the 2016 animated television series of the same name.[52]

Lego Trolls World Tour: A product line released in 2020 based on DreamWorks' Animation's Trolls franchise.[53][54]
