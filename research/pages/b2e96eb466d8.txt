Ja'Marr Chase's Stellar Performance Propels Bengals to Victory Over Steelers

November 17th, 2026
Sports

Author unknown
Cincinnati Bengals wide receiver Ja'Marr Chase delivered an exceptional performance on Sunday night, leading his team to a decisive 38-18 victory over the Pittsburgh Steelers. Chase caught 11 of 14 targets for an impressive 231 yards, marking the fourth 200-yard game of his career and his third against an AFC North rival. This standout performance contributed significantly to the Bengals' five-game winning streak and solidified their position atop the AFC North division.

Chase's recent surge in productivity has been remarkable. After a relatively slow start to the season, he has recorded at least 141 receiving yards in each of his last three games. His synergy with quarterback Joe Burrow has been instrumental in the Bengals' offensive success, with both players enhancing each other's performance and fantasy football value.

Looking ahead, Chase is poised to continue his dominant form as the Bengals prepare to face the Washington Commanders in Week 11. Given his current trajectory, he remains a high-end WR1 in fantasy football and a pivotal player in Cincinnati's pursuit of postseason success.
