Ja'Marr Chase debuts perfect Joe Burrow impression

 Originally posted on
 Larry Brown Sports
 | 
 By Grey Papke
 | 
 Last updated Nov 19, 2026 6:11 PM ET

 Add us as a preferred source on Google

 Cincinnati Bengals wide receiver Ja’Marr Chase “stood in” as Joe Burrow on Thursday in epic fashion. 

Burrow would normally meet with the media on Thursday, but the quarterback was ill, so the Bengals opted to move Chase’s media availability up by a day. Chase took full advantage by wearing a beanie and posing as Burrow to open his press conference.

“I’m quarterback today,” Chase joked.

Chase even batted away a question expertly about whether he, as Burrow, deserved MVP honors. The wide receiver said he was content to let everyone else talk about that.

Joe Burrow missed his press conference due to illness, so Ja’Marr Chase stepped in, wore Burrow’s jersey, and answered a few questions while impersonating his quarterback

The two former LSU stars maintain a very strong relationship, and it certainly helps that Burrow makes sure Chase gets more than his fair share of targets. Similarly, Chase knows Burrow well, and saw the quarterback’s season coming in a certain sense.

Burrow is, in fact, putting together an MVP-caliber season. He has thrown for 2,795 yards and a league-leading 27 touchdowns so far this season for the Bengals.
