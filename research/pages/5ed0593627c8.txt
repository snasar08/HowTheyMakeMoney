List of tennis stats

The ATP Tour is the modern top-level men's professional tennis circuit. It was introduced in 1990 and it is administered by the Association of Tennis Professionals (ATP). All the records listed here are only for the players who played most of their careers on the ATP Tour and they are based on official ATP data.[1] The names of active players appear in boldface. However, no boldface is used in lists exclusively for active players.
