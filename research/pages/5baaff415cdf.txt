Samsung’s Intelligent Eyewear to be released in the US and South Korea on 10th December

By Estuti Bajpai

 Published on November 13, 2026, 1:17 PM IST 5 min read

 Reading Mode Exit Reading Mode Add us as a preferred source on Google 

The South Korean tech giant Samsung has announced that its Intelligent Eyewear is set to be released in the US and South Korea on 10th December, and pre-orders have already started.

Samsung’s Intelligent Eyewear

The collection combines Samsung’s mobile engineering expertise with 30 distinctive designs from Gentle Monster and Warby Parker for comfortable, stylish everyday wear. Responsibly designed with everyone’s comfort in mind, the intelligent eyewear has controls that put the user in charge and has privacy protections for both the wearer and people around them.

Gentle Monster and Warby Parker bring their design identities to a combined lineup of 30 designs, offering customers a wide range of frame shapes, colours, sizes, and lens treatments to match their personal style.

Gentle Monster brings bold, disruptive aesthetics, while Warby Parker offers timeless designs with a refined appeal.

Weighing around 41g, the eyewear was carefully designed to balance weight, structural strength, and comfort, making it suitable for extended use throughout the day. With Gemini built in, the intelligent eyewear provides intuitive, hands-free experiences:

Explore and understand surroundings: Users can ask questions about what they see — such as identifying an unfamiliar landmark or learning about an object — continuing the conversation naturally with follow-up questions.

Enhance daily productivity: The intelligent eyewear helps users turn information into action. For example, a user can capture a whiteboard during a meeting, ask Gemini to summarise the key takeaways and save them directly into Samsung Notes.

Translate and navigate on the go: Users can translate a restaurant menu or find their way around unfamiliar places with turn-by-turn directions from Google Maps.

Stay connected, hands-free: The intelligent eyewear allows users to effortlessly stay connected. It can automatically prioritise, group, and summarise notifications based on context, read important updates aloud and enable natural voice responses. Whether making calls or responding to messages while their hands are occupied, users can stay connected on the go.

A 12MP HDR camera provides visual context for Gemini while supporting high-resolution photos and clear video recording. Open-ear speakers and a four-microphone array enable clear voice interactions, calls and audio playback.

The intelligent eyewear is powered by the Snapdragon AR1 Gen 1 platform, boasts up to 9 hours of battery life on a single charge, and up to 72 hours with a fully charged charging case. It utilises a split-computing system to offload a portion of the processing load to the connected device.

Responsible design is built into the practical experience:

Clear recording indicators: When capturing images or recording video for your gallery, an LED indicates that the camera is active, while the wearer simultaneously receives a visual notification via an inner light and a sound. Built-in safeguards are designed to disable camera capture if the external LED is covered or not functioning properly.

Considerate audio design: Open-ear speakers are designed to minimise sound leakage while helping wearers remain aware of their surroundings. In conversations with Gemini Live, surrounding sounds, including other people’s voices, are filtered to help protect bystander privacy.

User control and data privacy: Users retain control over both app-specific permissions and their Gemini activity. Visuals collected by Gemini are not used for AI model training or ad targeting. When users ask Gemini about what they’re looking at, the intelligent eyewear is designed to automatically redact bystanders’ faces before responding.

Responsible use: The intelligent eyewear is designed for users aged 18 and over, with age verification required for device activation and app downloads.

Availability

Gentle Monster Intelligent Eyewear and Warby Parker Intelligent Eyewear will be available in select markets beginning Dec. 10, 2026. Both collections will launch in the U.S., while Gentle Monster Intelligent Eyewear will also be available in Korea.

Pre-orders for both collections begin today, ahead of general availability. Customers will be able to experience and purchase the intelligent eyewear across Warby Parker’s retail and online channels, with purchases also available through Gentle Monster’s official online store. Hands-on demonstrations will also be available at select Samsung retail locations from Dec. 10.

In the U.S., Warby Parker Intelligent Eyewear will start at $395, while Gentle Monster Intelligent Eyewear will range from $449 to $489, depending on the model.
